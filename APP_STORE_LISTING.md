## Description (4000 max)
ModTok is your smart wardrobe. Add the clothes you own, style them into outfits, share your looks, and sell the pieces you no longer wear, all in one app.

ADD YOUR CLOSET
• Photograph your clothes or import them from your photos
• Sort by category, season and colour
• See your whole wardrobe at a glance

STYLE OUTFITS
• Swipe through tops, bottoms and shoes to build a look
• Save your favourite outfits and organise them into collections

SHARE YOUR STYLE
• Post your looks as stories
• Tag items you're selling so followers can shop them directly

SELL WHAT YOU DON'T WEAR
• List an item from your closet in a few taps
• Get paid securely through Stripe; ModTok keeps a 10% fee on the sale price
• Track every order from payment to shipping to delivery

BUY PRE-LOVED FASHION
• Browse clothing listed by other ModTok members
• Pay securely by card through Stripe
• Follow your order with shipping and tracking updates

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Report listings and stories, block users, and delete your account at any time from Settings

Marketplace buying and selling is currently available for U.S. shipping addresses and payments in USD.

**Notes for the reviewer** (paste as-is):
> ModTok is a wardrobe and peer-to-peer marketplace for physical, used clothing. Payments for physical goods are processed by Stripe (App Review Guideline 3.1.3(e)); there are no digital goods or subscriptions. Users can report listings and stories, block users (Guideline 1.2), and delete their account in Profile → Settings → Delete my account (Guideline 5.1.1(v)). The demo account has sample closet items and outfits so all features can be reviewed without making a purchase.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label", App Privacy tab)
Data collected, all **linked to the user**, **not used for tracking**:
- **Contact Info:** Email address, Name, Physical address (buyers' shipping address)
- **User Content:** Photos, Other user content (listings, stories)
- **Identifiers:** User ID
- **Purchases:** Purchase history
- **Financial Info:** handled by Stripe. Select **Payment info** and note it's collected by the payment processor
