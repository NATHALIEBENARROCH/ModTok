## Tomorrow, in this order
1. **Check the build arrived.** App Store Connect → ModTok → **TestFlight** tab. Or expo.dev → modtok → Submissions (green ✓).
2. **Test on your iPhone.** Install **TestFlight** from the App Store, open ModTok, and tap through everything.
3. **Take 3–5 screenshots** on your iPhone: closet, outfit builder, stories, marketplace, sell. Send them to Claude to resize.
4. **Create a test account** in the app for Apple's reviewer (new email + password) and add a few clothes.
5. **Stripe:** click **Verify now** (phone SMS), then open **Connect** in the left menu and send Claude a screenshot.
6. With Claude: select the build, upload the screenshots, enter the test login, then **Add for Review**.
