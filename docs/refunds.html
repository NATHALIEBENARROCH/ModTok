ModTok Refund and Returns Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

ModTok is a marketplace where individuals buy and sell physical clothing and accessories. Each item is usually one of a kind and is sold by another ModTok member, not by ModTok. This policy explains when buyers can get their money back.

1. Before the item ships

A buyer receives a full refund, including shipping, if:

  • the seller cancels the order before shipping it, or
  • the seller does not add a carrier and tracking number within three business days after payment.

Sellers can refund an unshipped order themselves from My Sales in the app. Buyers whose order has not shipped after three business days can contact nb26@me.com, and ModTok will cancel and refund it.

2. After the item is delivered

Sales are final once an item is delivered, except when the item:

  • never arrived, even though tracking shows it was shipped;
  • is materially different from the listing (wrong item, wrong size, or damage or defects that were not disclosed); or
  • is counterfeit.

In these cases, email nb26@me.com within three days after delivery with your order number, a short explanation and clear photos. Please do not confirm delivery in the app until you have checked the item.

If ModTok approves the claim, the buyer may be asked to return the item to the seller with tracking. The seller pays return shipping when the item was not as described. Once the return is delivered, or if no return is needed, the buyer receives a full refund.

Changing your mind, or an item not fitting when the listing showed the correct size, is not a reason for a refund unless the seller agrees.

3. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. They usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank. When a refund is approved, the seller's payout for that order is reversed.

4. Payment disputes

If you open a dispute or chargeback with your bank, Stripe and your card issuer handle it. Buyers and sellers must provide the evidence requested. Please contact nb26@me.com first; most problems can be solved faster directly.

5. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights you have under the law where you live.
