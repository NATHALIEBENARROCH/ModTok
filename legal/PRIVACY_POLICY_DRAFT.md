ModTok helps people organize wardrobes, create and share outfits and stories, list physical clothing and accessories for sale, buy listed items, arrange shipping, and track marketplace orders. By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and profile information

We collect information you provide when creating or maintaining an account, such as your email address, display name, username, profile photo, biography, authentication identifiers, and account preferences. If you sign in through Apple, Google, or another authentication provider, we receive the identifiers and profile information that provider makes available according to your settings.

### 1.2 Wardrobe, outfit, story, and marketplace content

We collect content you upload or create, including clothing photos, worn-look photos, outfit combinations, captions, categories, colors, seasons, occasions, tags, brands, sizes, item condition, listing descriptions, prices, shipping charges, stories, and listing tags. Some content is private to your wardrobe; content you intentionally publish as a story, profile element, or marketplace listing can be visible to other users.

### 1.3 Marketplace transaction and shipping information

When you buy or sell an item, we collect information necessary to process and administer the transaction, including the listing, buyer and seller account identifiers, item price, shipping charge, platform fee, order status, carrier, tracking number, refund or dispute status, and timestamps. We collect the buyer’s recipient name and U.S. shipping address so the seller can fulfill the order. Sellers must use shipping information only to complete the applicable ModTok order.

### 1.4 Seller identity and payout information

Seller onboarding, identity verification, payment acceptance, and payouts are provided by Stripe. Stripe may collect a seller’s legal name, date of birth, government identification information, tax information, business information, bank account or debit card details, and other verification information. ModTok receives limited status information from Stripe, such as the connected account identifier and whether verification, charges, and payouts are enabled. ModTok does not store complete bank account numbers or card numbers.

### 1.5 Payment information

Buyer payments are processed by Stripe through its secure payment interface. Stripe collects payment-card or other payment-method information. ModTok receives transaction identifiers, payment status, amount, currency, refund status, and limited payment-method details made available by Stripe. ModTok does not store complete payment-card numbers.

### 1.6 Reports, blocks, and support communications

We collect information when you report a listing or story, block another user, request support, dispute a transaction, request a refund, or communicate with us. This may include the reason for a report, related content and transaction records, correspondence, and actions taken.

### 1.7 Device and usage information

We may collect technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate location derived from an IP address, crash records, diagnostic data, access times, and interactions with Service features. We do not collect precise device location unless a future feature clearly requests permission and this Privacy Policy is updated.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to provide and secure accounts; store wardrobes and outfits; publish stories and marketplace listings at your direction; process purchases, seller onboarding, payments, payouts, shipping, refunds, disputes, and customer support; prevent duplicate sales of one-of-a-kind items; detect fraud and misuse; enforce our Terms of Use and marketplace rules; moderate reported content; honor blocks; communicate service and transaction updates; improve reliability and usability; comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations; and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where required by law, we rely on one or more legal grounds such as performance of a contract, consent, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Service. You may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis, but withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.

## 4. Public Content and Marketplace Visibility

Marketplace listings and stories are intended to be discoverable by other signed-in users. Do not include a home address, phone number, payment credentials, government identifier, or other sensitive information in a public caption, photo, profile, or listing description. Blocking a user removes that user’s listings and stories from your experience, but it does not necessarily delete copies previously seen, saved, or lawfully retained for safety and dispute handling.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the Service, the purposes described above, and applicable legal obligations. Active account and content data is generally retained while the account remains open. Expired stories may be removed from user-facing views after 24 hours but may remain temporarily in backups, logs, fraud-prevention records, or moderation records.

When an account is deleted, ModTok removes or de-identifies the profile, wardrobe, outfits, stories, listings, and other personal content unless retention is required or permitted by law. Completed order records may be retained in anonymized or restricted form for tax, accounting, chargeback, fraud, dispute, and legal obligations. Accounts with unfinished paid orders, refunds, or disputes may need those transactions resolved before deletion can be completed. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before routine deletion.

You may change certain profile and content settings in the app, unlist active items, delete eligible content, block users, and manage communication permissions through your device or account settings.
