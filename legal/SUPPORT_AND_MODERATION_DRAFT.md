## Public Support Page Copy

### Contact ModTok Support

ModTok helps people organize wardrobes, share outfits, and buy or sell physical clothing through our peer-to-peer marketplace.

For account access, orders, shipping, seller payouts, refunds, privacy requests, or safety concerns, email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**. Include the email associated with your ModTok account and, for transaction questions, the order number. Do not email complete payment-card numbers, bank details, passwords, or government identification documents.

| Request | Information to include | Initial response target |
|---|---|---|
| Order or shipping issue | Order number, item name, summary, and any tracking information | Within two business days |
| Item not as described | Order number, explanation, and clear photos | Within two business days |
| Seller payout issue | Seller email and Stripe onboarding or payout status shown in the app | Within two business days |
| Story or listing safety report | Account or listing, reason, and relevant screenshots if available | Within one business day |
| Privacy or account deletion | Account email and the request | Within five business days |
| Security concern | Account email and a concise description, without passwords | As soon as practicable |

### Report and Block

You can report a marketplace listing from the listing’s product page. You can report a story from the story options menu. Reports are reviewed against the ModTok Terms of Use and community rules.

You can also block another user from the same options. Blocking removes that user’s marketplace listings and stories from your experience. If there is an immediate threat or emergency, contact local emergency services; an in-app report is not an emergency service.

### Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your ModTok account in the app under **Profile → Settings → Delete my account**. The app will ask you to confirm. Your personal profile, wardrobe, outfits, stories, and listings will be removed or de-identified, subject to legally required transaction-record retention.

If your account has an unfinished paid order, refund, or dispute, ModTok may require that transaction to be resolved before deletion. Contact **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** if you need assistance.

### Marketplace Order Help

Sellers should ship paid orders using the address displayed in **My Sales**, then enter the carrier and tracking number. Buyers can follow progress under **My Purchases**. Buyers should report non-delivery, significant undisclosed damage, suspected counterfeit goods, or a materially incorrect item promptly through **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. The time needed for the credit to appear is determined by Stripe, the card network, and the buyer’s financial institution.

## Internal Moderation Procedure

ModTok should assign a monitored support inbox and a responsible person before App Store submission. Reports should be triaged by severity, connected to the relevant user and content, and documented with the action and timestamp.

| Priority | Examples | Target action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Credible physical threat, child exploitation, trafficking, imminent harm | Preserve evidence, disable access as appropriate, and escalate to law enforcement or emergency channels where legally required |
| High | Counterfeit or stolen goods, targeted harassment, doxxing, repeated fraud | Review within one business day; remove content or restrict account when supported |
| Standard | Spam, misleading description, ordinary policy violation | Review within two business days and communicate the outcome where appropriate |
| Transaction | Non-shipment, item materially not as described, refund request | Preserve order evidence, contact the parties, and decide under the published refund policy |

A report should not automatically establish wrongdoing. Review the listing or story, account history, transaction records, and evidence; limit access when necessary to protect users; communicate decisions in plain language; and keep only the records needed for safety, disputes, and legal compliance.
